01

What Best CEO is, in 60 seconds

Most marketing tools give you a dashboard and leave the thinking to you. Best CEO works the other way around.

You give it one thing, your website URL, and it returns a deep audit of your site: technical SEO, performance, accessibility, AI visibility, keywords, backlinks, social evidence, competitors, readability, and brand identity among them. Evidence is drawn from Lighthouse lab tests, Moz data, automated accessibility checks, and AI-readiness checks; Search Console connects separately for search-performance features. Detected issues are ranked by severity, and supported findings include implementation guidance or fix snippets. Deep analysis can take a few minutes.

Then it stops being a report and starts being a department. Eight AI executives (a CEO, CMO, CRO, CFO, COO, CPO, CTO, and CXO) share one context: your report, your brand voice, your competitors. Ask them for role-specific help, and with eligible paid access, schedule briefs that summarize your workspace evidence and priorities in Slack, Google Chat, or the dashboard.

The mental model that will serve you best: you didn't buy a tool. You hired an org chart. The rest of this guide shows you how to put it to work.

Best CEO uses GLM models through 0G to power its AI: the reports, the executive conversations, and the content drafts. Eight executives work from one shared company context, and website analysis runs in the background, so you can keep exploring the app while your report takes shape.

02

Your first session

This is the loop that builds trust in the product. Do it before you read anything else.

STEP 1

Explore or sign up

The demo opens a read-only sample workspace without signing up, good for exploring. Running your own analyses, connecting integrations, and saving changes require an account. The free plan at best.ceo/signup includes website analysis up to three times per month, no card required.

STEP 2

Analyze your website

Set the website for your workspace and start an analysis. Scan your site and review the findings when the analysis is ready; deep analysis can take a few minutes.

STEP 3

Read the findings

Review the issues detected, their evidence, and the recommended next steps. Coverage depends on the pages inspected and the data available. Some supported findings include implementation guidance or fix snippets.

STEP 4

Choose and review a fix

Pick a recommendation that fits, review it, and apply it through the supported workflow or your usual site-editing process. Missing meta descriptions and absent schema markup are common first finds.

STEP 5

Return to assess progress

Review your report and comparable scan history in your workspace. Check the scan date before treating a result as a new measurement: a repeat request can reuse a completed scan from the last 24 hours. Use a fresh, comparable scan when assessing a change.

That's the whole loop: scan, read, fix, re-measure. Everything else in Best CEO builds on it.

03

Understanding your report

One scan produces a full-length intelligence report, from raw evidence to suggested next steps. Sections populate as evidence is available: a given scan may show partial or no data for some of them. Here's the map, and how to read it.

First: how to trust what you read

The provenance system

Best CEO distinguishes how each piece of context and analysis was produced. Sections built from checks are marked measured; sections where the AI interprets that evidence are marked AI-synthesized / advisory. Scores carry confidence labels, and where evidence is unavailable the report says so rather than guessing. Company-profile fields carry their own source labels. Read these first: they tell you whether you're looking at an observation, your own confirmed input, an estimate, or an interpretation.

VERIFIED
Confirmed by your workspace input or review. It records your confirmation, not independent proof.
INFERRED
Estimated from the scan and available context. Review, then confirm or correct.
MISSING
Needs your input before Best should trust it. Fill these in early, they sharpen everything downstream.

Do first: open your company profile, confirm the inferred fields (business model, content strategy, keywords, company stage), and fill in anything marked missing. Five minutes here upgrades the quality of every future analysis and brief.

The scoreboard

Threat assessment matrix

The top of the report rolls the scanned evidence up into headline dials such as SEO, Social evidence, AI visibility, Readability, Visual coverage, and GEO, each scored 0-100 with a confidence tag where evidence is available. This is your at-a-glance health check. The rest of the report is the evidence behind each dial.

The evidence sections: what was measured

SEO intelligence

Do first

Detected on-page issues ranked critical → warning → info → passing, tagged by category, with estimated effort to help you prioritize (estimates don't guarantee implementation time or impact). Start with the critical items; supported findings include guidance or fix snippets.

Performance & speed

Available Lighthouse lab results for mobile and desktop, plus Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) and supporting metrics (FCP, TBT) where available. Synthetic lab measurements, not field data from real users, and evidence can be incomplete on some scans.

AI visibility / GEO

Site-readiness signals combined with available provider evidence: extractability, schema and FAQ presence, and a crawler policy check with citation/search crawlers kept separate from training crawlers. The base audit distinguishes a configured Perplexity check from a general web-presence proxy; the proxy does not directly test other assistants. Review access for the crawlers relevant to your goals: access is one contribution to the score, not a guarantee of citations.

Rival dossiers

Confirmed workspace rivals stay attached to future analyses; newly discovered candidates arrive with match scores and evidence, flagged "verify before saving." Review candidates before saving, accuracy here compounds.

Keyword & backlink intelligence

Keyword rows split honestly between provider-backed metrics and explicitly estimated discovery rows (a missing metric is not zero demand), plus Moz-backed authority data: Domain Authority, backlink and referring-domain counts, and top referring domains, with coverage limits stated when provider data is partial.

Also in the report

Social evidence (discovered profiles and available platform-specific metrics, principally X/Twitter), a sampled site-pages table, a qualitative and deliberately unscored E-E-A-T signal inventory, readability analysis judged against your audience, automated accessibility checks referencing selected WCAG 2.1 criteria (not a conformance audit), and your extracted visual identity (read-only, set your Brand Guide separately).

The synthesis: from evidence to action

Executive brief & action lists

An AI-synthesized summary of what was measured, what scored where, and which claims were withheld for lack of evidence, followed by day-1 actions and a weekly checklist with estimated time and priority per fix. Confirm each fix with a fresh, comparable re-scan.

Projections & growth trajectory

Your scoreboard

Projected score movement per fix (score estimates, not traffic, ranking, or revenue forecasts) alongside your measured score history from comparable stored scans. Check scan dates: a repeat request can reuse a completed scan from the last 24 hours, and fresh, comparable scans are what make a real before/after.

Positioning, opportunities & previews

An interpretive competitive narrative backed by a like-for-like measured crawl comparison against rivals, AI-synthesized quick wins and long-term plays labeled by impact and effort, and simulated skill previews generated from your report context, illustrations of possible use, not measured findings.

Put it on autopilot

This is where Best CEO earns its tagline. Setup takes a few minutes.

STEP 1

Connect your workspace

From the dashboard, connect Slack or Google Chat and choose a briefs channel. A dedicated channel like #marketing-briefs keeps the signal clean.

STEP 2

Confirm your company context

Check that your competitors list, tracked keywords, and company profile are confirmed. Scheduled briefs draw on this workspace evidence.

STEP 3

Choose your schedules

With eligible paid access, choose from the schedule templates available in the app, a daily brief and weekly digest are the usual starting points, and select the delivery destination shown in your account. Scheduled briefs summarize available workspace evidence and priorities. Ordinary Free access excludes scheduled AI; eligible active trials may include it.

STEP 4

Review your first brief

When your first scheduled brief arrives, skim it, and check delivery status in the dashboard if it doesn't. Briefs draw on analysis scores, comparable changes, approved direction, and completed actions: a summary of what your workspace knows, not a guaranteed overnight re-check of rivals and rankings. That first unprompted summary is when most users say the product clicks.

07

For developers

The REST API exposes a supported subset of Best CEO capabilities.

Create a key from Dashboard → Developer → API Keys (workspace-owner access and a Starter plan or higher required). Keys start with bestceo_ and the full key is shown once at creation. Authenticate with a Bearer header. The v1 authentication layer applies a limit of 100 requests per minute per key: a rate limit, not a scan allowance, and endpoint and plan quotas also apply.

Coming from CIB? Legacy cib_ keys are no longer accepted. Generate a new bestceo_ key from Dashboard → Developer → API Keys and swap it into your integrations before your next call.

Analysis requests are limited to the website configured for your workspace and may return a suitable completed scan from the last 24 hours instead of starting a new one. The v1 API covers a supported set of analysis, skill, and brand-mention operations; see the API documentation for the current interface.

GitHub users: with eligible plan access, GitHub connected, and an authorized repository selected, the workspace owner can review a supported fix and open a pull request. Opening a pull request does not itself merge or deploy the change.

08

A suggested first month

A suggested sequence, not a schedule: adapt it to what your report shows. Fresh, comparable scans are your measuring stick throughout.

Week 1: Review and fix

Review the report, confirm useful company context (the inferred profile fields), and choose feasible fixes from the findings. Repair or redirect broken links.

Top findings triaged and your first fixes shipped.

Week 2: Connect and schedule

Connect Google Search Console for supported search-performance features (search clicks, impressions, click-through rate, average position) once setup is complete and data is available. You need appropriate access to the property, connected credentials don't mean live data is ready immediately, and connecting doesn't change the base audit score. Confirm your competitor list, pick keywords to track, and select supported schedules if eligible.

Integrations connected and your first scheduled brief configured.

Week 3: Create and review

Generate a supported content output, running Blog Pipeline on your best keyword opportunity is a good start. Review the draft yourself for claims and brand fit, Fact Checker's analysis data helps you cross-check, then publish through your normal workflow.

First reviewed piece published.

Week 4: Measure and decide

Run a fresh scan and compare comparable measurements in Growth trajectory, checking scan dates. Read your latest digest, then ask Best CEO: "Given the last 30 days, what should we prioritize next month?"

A month-two plan grounded in fresh, comparable measurements.

By day 30 you'll have triaged the foundation, set up the routines, shipped reviewed content, and, most importantly, you'll have comparable measurements to judge what changed.
