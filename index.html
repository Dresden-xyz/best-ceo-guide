01

What Best CEO is — in 60 seconds

Most marketing tools give you a dashboard and leave the thinking to you. Best CEO works the other way around.

You give it one thing — your website URL — and in under two minutes it produces what a competent CMO would need a week to assemble: a full audit of your site across 15 dimensions, scored against Lighthouse, Moz, WCAG 2.2, Google Search Console, and live queries to the major AI models. Every issue is ranked by severity and paired with a copy-paste fix.

Then it stops being a report and starts being a department. Eight AI executives — a CEO, CMO, CRO, CFO, COO, CPO, CTO, and CXO — share one context: your report, your brand voice, your competitors. They run on schedules you set, watching rivals, drafting content, and briefing you each weekday morning in Slack, Google Chat, or the dashboard.

The mental model that will serve you best: you didn't buy a tool. You hired an org chart. The rest of this guide shows you how to put it to work.

That's the whole confidence loop: scan, see, fix, verify. Everything else in Best CEO is this loop, automated and running while you sleep.

03

Understanding your report

One scan produces a full-length intelligence report — more than twenty sections, from raw evidence to a prioritized action plan. Here's the map, and how to read it.

First — how to trust what you read

The provenance system

Best CEO labels every fact in your company profile by where it came from, and every analysis section by how it was produced. Sections built from live checks are marked measured; sections where the AI interprets that evidence are marked AI-synthesized / advisory. Scores carry confidence labels, and where evidence is unavailable the report says so and withholds the claim rather than guessing. Read these labels first — they tell you how much weight each number can bear.

VERIFIED
Confirmed by your workspace input or review. Safe to act on.
INFERRED
Estimated from the scan and available context. Review, then confirm or correct.
MISSING
Needs your input before Best should trust it. Fill these in early — they sharpen everything downstream.

Do first: open your company profile, confirm the inferred fields (business model, content strategy, keywords, company stage), and fill in anything marked missing. Five minutes here upgrades the quality of every future analysis and brief.

The scoreboard

Threat assessment matrix

The top of the report is a dial-per-dimension summary: SEO, Social evidence, AI/GEO, Readability, Visual coverage, and GEO, each scored 0–100 with a confidence tag. This is your at-a-glance health check — the rest of the report is the evidence behind each dial.

The evidence sections — what was measured

SEO intelligence

Do first

Every on-page issue found, ranked critical → warning → info → passing, tagged by category (meta tags, content structure, structured data), and stamped with an estimated fix time — so a 5-minute critical fix never hides behind a 2-hour warning.

Do first: clear every critical item; most have copy-paste fixes.

Performance & speed

Google PageSpeed / Lighthouse lab audits for both mobile and desktop, across performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO — plus Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, FCP, INP, TBT) graded against Good/Poor thresholds. The report is explicit that these are synthetic lab measurements, not field data from real users.

Do first: chase any vital graded Poor; those drag both rankings and conversions.

AI visibility / GEO

A letter grade plus the evidence behind it: how many pages were sampled for citation coverage, your content extractability score, schema and FAQ presence, and a crawler policy check showing exactly which AI bots your site allows or blocks — separated into citation/search crawlers (the ones that get you cited in answers) and training crawlers (a separate policy decision). It also captures your branded SERP position: where your own domain and third-party profiles rank when someone searches your brand.

Do first: make sure you aren't accidentally blocking the citation/search crawlers — that single robots.txt line can zero out your AI visibility.

Social intelligence

Observed-evidence scoring of your social presence: which profiles are verifiably linked from your site, activity metrics (followers, posting cadence, average engagement, account age), your top-performing post, and community mentions measured from public sources like Hacker News and Reddit. The report separates what it observed from what it couldn't verify — it never asserts account ownership it hasn't seen.

Rival dossiers

Two lists: workspace rivals you've confirmed, which stay attached to every future analysis, and ranked candidates discovered in this scan, each with a match score and supporting evidence, flagged "verify before saving."

Do first: promote real competitors to workspace rivals and dismiss false positives — this list feeds Competitor Radar and your morning briefs, so accuracy compounds.

Site pages

Sitemap enumeration plus a sampled page-by-page table: word counts, H1 and meta status, detected JSON-LD types, and row-level issues like thin content. The sample size is stated plainly, so you know what was and wasn't inspected.

E-E-A-T assessment

An inventory of inspectable Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness signals — identity structured data, linked knowledge bases, press pages, privacy/terms/contact destinations. Deliberately unscored: Google doesn't expose an E-E-A-T score, and the report won't invent one. Use it as a checklist of trust signals to add.

Readability analysis

Flesch Reading Ease with grade level, average sentence length, and word counts from your actual page prose — plus recommendations judged against your audience, not an arbitrary target. Complex vocabulary can be exactly right for a technical B2B reader; the report says so when it is.

Accessibility audit

Automated checks mapped to specific WCAG 2.2 success criteria (with level A/AA references), each finding explained in terms of the real users it affects. Clearly labeled as automated checks, not a full conformance audit.

Keyword intelligence

Keyword rows with search volume and difficulty — and an honest split: provider-backed rows show measured metrics, while discovery rows are explicitly marked as estimated so they never read like measured demand.

Do first: pick 5–10 provider-backed keywords to track; they seed Ranking Monitor and the SEO Content Engine.

Backlink intelligence

Moz-backed authority data: Domain Authority, backlink and referring-domain counts, link velocity, and your top referring domains ranked by their own authority. When the provider returns partial coverage, the report says so and tells you to treat counts as directional.

Visual identity

Your extracted brand palette and a typography fingerprint (heading, body, theme), each labeled with its provenance and confidence. This feeds the Brand Guide skill, so correcting it here keeps every generated asset on-brand.

The synthesis — from evidence to action

Executive brief

A plain-language, AI-synthesized summary of what was measured, what scored where, and — just as important — which claims were withheld because the evidence wasn't available. This is the paragraph to forward to your team.

Day-1 operations & weekly priority checklist

The highest-impact actions you can execute immediately, each with a validation target ("re-run the audit and confirm the finding clears") — plus a weekly checklist with estimated time and priority per fix. Nothing is done until a re-scan proves it.

What happens next

Projected score movement per fix — each row closes one measured gap and re-runs the same scoring formula that produced your current score. The report is explicit: these are score estimates, not traffic, ranking, or revenue forecasts. Connect analytics for a measured traffic baseline.

Growth trajectory

Your scoreboard

Your own measured score history, per dimension, plotted from stored scans under one consistent scoring method — nothing forecast, only readings. This is the engine behind the shareable report URL: every re-scan adds a data point, and the before/after writes itself.

Competitive positioning & rival-comparable crawl signals

An interpretive narrative of where you sit among confirmed rivals, backed by a like-for-like measured comparison: the identical lightweight check (AI-crawler access plus structured-data presence) run on your site and each rival in the same scan, so the bars are actually comparable.

Backlink & content opportunities

AI-synthesized plans split into quick wins and long-term plays, each labeled by impact and effort, and each anchored to a measured finding with a recommended validation step before you commit resources.

Agent capability preview & "A Day With Your Agent"

Sample outputs generated from your own report context — what Content Strategy, SEO Engine, Competitor Radar, KOL Map, and other skills would produce once live — plus a simulated day-one timeline. Clearly labeled as previews, not measured findings: a test drive of the department before you turn it on.

Put it on autopilot

This is where Best CEO earns its tagline. The setup takes about five minutes.

STEP 1

Connect your workspace

From the dashboard, connect Slack or Google Chat and choose a briefs channel. A dedicated channel like #marketing-briefs keeps the signal clean.

STEP 2

Confirm your company context

Check that your competitors list (from the Competition tab), tracked keywords (from the Keywords tab), and Brand Guide are all set. Every scheduled skill draws on these.

STEP 3

Enable the schedules

Turn on Daily Brief (weekday mornings), Weekly Digest (Mondays), and Ranking Monitor (hourly checks, alerts only when something moves). On Pro, all cron schedules are available — add Daily Tweet Drafts, Competitor Radar sweeps, or anything else on the cadence you want.

STEP 4

Do nothing

Tomorrow morning there will be a brief in your channel summarizing overnight competitor moves, ranking changes, and your current score. That moment — marketing intelligence arriving without being asked for — is when most users say the product clicks.

07

For developers

Everything the dashboard does is reachable over REST.

Create a key from Dashboard → Developer → API Keys. Keys start with bestceo_, can't be retrieved after creation, and require a Starter plan or higher. Authenticate with a Bearer header; the rate limit is 100 requests per minute per key.

Trigger a scan programmatically:

# Create a new analysis
curl -X POST "https://best.ceo/api/v1/analyses" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer bestceo_your_api_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"url": "https://yoursite.com"}'

The v1 API covers listing and creating analyses, fetching full analysis detail, listing all skills, and searching brand mentions across Reddit and Hacker News. Full reference at best.ceo/docs.

Your first 30 days

A week-by-week plan. Your shareable report URL is the scoreboard throughout — real numbers, not promises.

Week 1 — Fix

Work the Findings tab top-down and clear your ten highest-severity issues using the copy-paste fixes. Fix or redirect every broken link. Set up your Brand Guide.

Overall score visibly up on your report URL.

Week 2 — Connect and seed

Connect Google Search Console and GA4 so Keyword Intelligence and Analytics run on your real data. Confirm your competitor list, pick 5–10 keywords for Ranking Monitor, and connect Slack with all three schedules on.

Your first Daily Brief arrives without you asking.

Week 3 — Ship

Run Blog Pipeline end to end on your best keyword opportunity, pass the draft through Content QA and Content Humanizer, and publish. Let Daily Tweet Drafts feed your social queue.

First piece of system-produced content live.

Week 4 — Review and decide

Read Monday's Weekly Digest. Open the Growth trajectory section — your measured score history per dimension — and compare against day zero. Ask Best CEO: "Given the last 30 days, what should we double down on next month?"

A written plan for month two, grounded in your own before/after.

By day 30 you'll have fixed the foundation, automated the watching, shipped real content, and — most importantly — you'll have a verifiable record of what changed. That's the confidence this product is built to earn.
